Controls
Move / turnLeft stick walks (click to sprint). Right stick turns smoothly. Click right stick to crouch.
Draw pistolReach to your right hip and squeeze grip. Let go of grip to holster it.
ShootTrigger. Hold the gun with your other hand too for less recoil.
ReloadA drops the magazine. Grab a new one from your left hip pouch, push it into the grip, then press B or pull the slide back.
Jump / menuX jumps. Y opens the pause menu (save, settings).
Doors & thingsSqueeze grip on the door handle and pull. Point and pull trigger on menus.
Browser previewWASD move, mouse look (click the scene), left click shoot, R reload, E use, Space jump, C crouch, Esc menu.
