GORILLA GRINDER

Stage 1 · Sandbox & Gore

You broke out of the lab: two metres of pale scarred muscle, black claws, broken restraints still on your wrists. The warehouse is full of gorillas. Gorilla Tag movement, layered gore, Meta Quest 3.

  • Move Gorilla Tag style: slap the floor and walls and push off · Climb hold Grip on a wall, claws dig in
  • Pounce hold A or X to charge, release to leap at them
  • Punch hold Grip for a fist and swing · Grab press Grip on a gorilla, organ or body part
  • Throw swing and let go · Slam smash a held gorilla into the floor or a wall
  • Rip grab two parts and pull: arm or leg off, head off (the spine comes with it), left + right side rips them in half, top + bottom splits the waist
  • Crush a head both hands on the head and squeeze slowly, or hold Trigger with one hand
  • Heart open hand, stab into the chest, Grip to grab it, pull it out, Trigger to crush
  • Eat hold a head, organ or limb to your mouth · Spine whip grab the spine and swing
  • Spawn slap the console buttons on your right · Menu Y or B

Armored guards: break the helmet and vest with punches before you can rip them. Room-scale only, turn with your body. made by Noah Young.
