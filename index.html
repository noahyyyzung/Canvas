CRIMSON

Stage 1 · Combat Core — Kessler Alley

No powers. No weapons. Just your hands. Four waves of the empire's street muscle in one rain-soaked alley, for Meta Quest 3.

  • Punch squeeze the trigger for a fist and swing. Your hands are solid and stop on bodies
  • Elbow strike with a bent arm close to your body
  • Guard both hands up by your face; block fists with hands or forearms
  • Push press an open hand into a chest · Dodge lean or step
  • Grab grip on a collar, arm or wrist · Throw swing and let go
  • Over-the-shoulder two-hand grab, pull up and back over your head
  • Move left stick · Turn right stick · Jump swing both arms up · Pause Y / B

Out of a fight, swing your arms while holding the stick to run faster. When enemies are close, the stick moves you so your arms stay free. Stand straight and press Calibrate Height in the menu.
