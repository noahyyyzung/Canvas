WELCOME TO PALMERA BAY · STAGE 1
VR (Quest)
Left stick: walk · Click left stick: sprint · Right stick: snap turn
Right trigger: shoot · A: drop mag · Grab (grip) the pouch on your left hip for a new mag, push it into the bottom of the gun
Grab a car's door handle (driver side) to get in. If someone's driving, you yank them out (2 stars)
In car: grab the steering wheel with grip · Right trigger: gas · Left trigger: brake/reverse · X: horn · B / Y: get out · Click left stick: re-center seat

Desktop
Click to lock mouse · WASD move · Shift sprint · Click shoot · R reload · E enter/exit car · In car: W/S gas/brake, A/D steer, H horn
